What is CMMC?

The Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) 2.0 is a DoD framework requiring defense contractors to implement and verify cybersecurity practices that protect Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) and Federal Contract Information (FCI).

🔐

Protect CUI & FCI

Safeguard sensitive federal contract and controlled unclassified information across the defense industrial base.

✅

Third-Party Verified

Level 2 and Level 3 require assessments by C3PAOs (Third-Party Assessment Organizations) or government assessors.

🏗️

Built on NIST SP 800-171

CMMC 2.0 aligns directly with NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 practices for protecting CUI in nonfederal systems.

CMMC Maturity Levels

CMMC 2.0 defines three progressive maturity levels, each building on the previous with additional practices and rigor.

Level 1

Foundational

17 Practices 6 Domains Self-Assessment

Basic cyber hygiene for all DoD contractors handling FCI. Annual self-assessment with executive affirmation required.

  • Protect FCI in company systems
  • Basic access control
  • User identification & authentication
  • Incident response basics
Target: All contractors handling FCI
Level 2

Advanced

110 Practices 14 Domains C3PAO Assessment

Advanced cyber practices for contractors handling CUI. Triennial third-party assessments for critical programs.

  • Full NIST SP 800-171 alignment
  • Multi-factor authentication
  • Privileged access management
  • Audit & accountability
  • Configuration management
Target: Contractors handling CUI
Level 3

Expert

110+ Practices 14 Domains Gov. Led Assessment

Expert-level practices for contractors supporting the highest priority programs. DIBCAC government-led assessments.

  • NIST SP 800-172 additions
  • Advanced persistent threat protection
  • Proactive threat hunting
  • Advanced monitoring & detection
  • Supply chain risk management
Target: Critical national security programs
